About Me

Professional Profile

I am a garment industry professional with extensive experience in knit garment cutting operations and cutting department management. My experience covers production planning, fabric consumption, marker efficiency, manpower management, cutting quality and delivery coordination.

I have worked with Lectra and Bullmer automatic cutting machines and Boke and Lectra spreading machines. My technical knowledge includes fabric relaxation, fabric inspection, GSM, dia, shade variation, shrinkage and cut-panel quality.

I coordinate with CAD, Merchandising, Dyeing, Printing, Embroidery, Sewing and Quality teams to support production targets, minimize fabric wastage and maintain smooth factory operations.
