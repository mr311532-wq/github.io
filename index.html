About Me

I am an apparel manufacturing professional with extensive experience in cutting-room operations, particularly in knit and composite garment environments. My experience includes daily cutting plans, fabric consumption, marker coordination, spreading and auto-cutting operations, shade management, quality control, manpower utilization, and production reporting.

My professional approach is to improve fabric utilization, reduce avoidable wastage, maintain cutting quality, support sewing-line requirements, and coordinate effectively with CAD, merchandising, fabric, dyeing, and production teams.
